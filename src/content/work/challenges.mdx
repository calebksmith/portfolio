## Problem

Vimocity helps people move more during the workday. The product only works if
people come back — a single visit changes nothing. We needed something that made
movement social and repeatable, without turning it into a chore or a
surveillance tool.

## What I did

- Defined three challenge types: personal targets, team targets, and team
  leaderboards — so people could opt into competition or avoid it.
- Designed the flows across mobile and web, and integrated them with the desktop
  reminder app.
- Connected challenges to daily movement targets, so everyday activity counted
  toward something.
- Tested with customers and non-customers to check the mechanics motivated
  rather than pressured.

## Outcome

- Movement became a shared activity rather than an individual task.
- Daily reminders now ladder into a longer-term goal.
- The feature works across all three platforms from one set of rules.

> **Design note:** the hardest decision was making competition optional.
> Leaderboards motivate some people and alienate others, especially in a health
> context tied to work. Personal targets let people participate without being
> ranked.

## Problem

> **Draft, needs your input:** what wasn’t working before V-minder, and for
> whom? The comps suggest people at desks all day, where a once-a-day routine
> with a crew doesn’t fit. Then the goal, as “Our goal was…”.

> **Design challenge:** getting people to move during the workday without
> having to remember to open anything. We built V-minder around the
> notification rather than the window: the move arrives on its own, on the
> person’s schedule, with Watch, Snooze, and Dismiss on the notification
> itself.

## What I did

- **Let people set their own schedule.** Days, hours, and how often turn into
  an outcome as you set them: “your schedule will deliver 8 moves a day,” next
  to the recommended 6–8.
- **Made preferences show their effect.** Body areas, sitting or standing, and
  equipment each update a live count of the videos that still match.
- **Made every move count.** Four moves in a day complete your Daily 5, and
  they count toward any challenge you’ve joined, without a challenge screen on
  desktop at all.
- **Reused the shared sign-in.** V-minder signs in through the same
  [login](/work/login) webview as every other platform.

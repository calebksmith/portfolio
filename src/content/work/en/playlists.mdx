## Problem

Vimocity had a video library and no way to hand someone part of it. Safety
leaders run briefings and crew leaders run daily huddles, each with videos
chosen in advance and played for a group where not everyone has an account.
Our goal was to get the right videos in front of a crew, account or not.

> **Design challenge:** reaching people without an account, without giving up
> sign-ups entirely. We chose reach: shared playlists open on an ungated page,
> so content gets from a crew leader to a crew without a registration step.
> Sign-in sits on the page rather than in front of it, adding what only makes
> sense with an account: saving, watch status, and group logging.

## What I did

- **Added group logging.** One person logs a video or a Daily 5 for everyone
  who did it with them, so a crew doesn’t need ten phones out to get credit.
- **Designed embeds for intranets and tailboard systems.** They’re allowlisted
  by domain and tied to the playlist itself, so one edit updates every embed,
  from a crew leader’s iPad to the company intranet.
- **Designed a player for break rooms.** Autoplay, repeat, and full screen let
  a playlist loop on a break-room TV for people who never open the app.

April was also a challenge month, so not all of that rise is playlists.
Sharing by link was barely used; embeds are where sharing happened, with
screens playing for a room again and again.

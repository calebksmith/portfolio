## Problem

Vimocity had a library of videos and no way to hand someone part of it. People
could watch content, but they could not group it and pass the group on.

That was the job our stakeholders actually had. Safety leaders run safety
meetings and daily briefings; crew leaders run daily huddles. Each needs a set
of videos for a specific audience, chosen in advance and played in front of a
group — often a group where not everyone has a Vimocity account.

## What I did

- Designed playlists people build from anything in the library and share across
  their organization.
- Opened shared playlists on an ungated page. Anyone with the link can watch,
  with or without an account — a business decision to get content from a
  foreman to a crew without a registration step in between.
- Put sign-in on that page rather than in front of it. Signing in adds what only
  makes sense with an account: saving and sharing videos, watch status,
  member-only resources, and group logging.
- Group logging matters most here. One person logs a video or a Daily 5 for
  everyone who did it with them, so a crew does not need ten phones out to get
  credit.
- Designed an HTML embed for intranet sites and tailboard systems. It only runs
  on domains the organization has allowlisted, so a playlist cannot be lifted
  onto a page nobody approved.
- Connected each embed to its playlist rather than to a copy of it. Editing the
  playlist updates every embed, so a safety lead can change what plays on a
  foreman's iPad or the company intranet as often as they like.
- Gave the embed two forms: a player that rotates through the playlist on a
  schedule the owner sets, anywhere from daily to weekly, and a link out to the
  full ungated playlist page.
- Added autoplay, repeat, and full screen, so a playlist can run on a loop on a
  break-room TV. The content reaches people who never open the app or sit in
  the meeting — it is on while they take a break.

Sales uses the same tool to build playlists for prospects, tailored to their
industry or organization.

## Outcome

- **Saves grew more than 4x** from launch to their peak month.
- The share of active web users watching a video rose from 48% to 58% in the
  three months after launch. April was also a challenge month, so not all of
  that is playlists.
- Embedded playlists log thousands of launches a month from a few dozen
  devices — the pattern of a screen playing for a room, not one viewer each.
- Sharing by link was barely used. Embeds are where sharing actually happened.
